## 🔐 Logging In

1. Go to **yoursite.com/admin/login**
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. You'll be redirected to the admin dashboard

### Forgot your password?
- On the login page, click **"Forgot password?"**
- Enter your email and click **"Send Reset Link"**
- Check your inbox for the reset email and follow the link
- You'll be taken to a page where you can set a new password

---

Once logged in, you'll see a sidebar (desktop) or bottom navigation (mobile) with these sections:

## 📬 Managing Messages

When someone fills out the **Contact** form on your website, their message appears here.

- **View messages**: Click on any message to see the full details (name, email, message)
- **Mark as read**: Messages can be marked as read so you know which ones you've handled
- **Delete**: Remove messages you no longer need

## 🎥 Managing Portfolio

This is where you showcase your video/film projects.

### Adding a new project:
1. Click **"Add Project"**
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — Name of the project
   - **Category** — Type of work (e.g., "brand", "documentary", "wedding")
   - **Description** — Brief description of the project
   - **Video URL** — Link to the video (YouTube, Vimeo, etc.)
   - **Thumbnail** — Upload a cover image for the project
3. Click **Save**

### Editing a project:
- Click the **Edit** (pencil) icon on any project card
- Update the fields and save

### Deleting a project:
- Click the **Delete** (trash) icon
- The project will be removed from the website immediately

### Reordering:
- Projects are displayed by their **sort order** — lower numbers appear first

## ⭐ Managing Testimonials

Client testimonials appear on your website to build trust.

### Adding a testimonial:
1. Click **"Add Testimonial"**
2. Fill in:
   - **Client Name** — Who said it
   - **Role / Title** — Their title or relationship (e.g., "CEO, BrandX" or "Wedding Client")
   - **Quote** — What they said about your work
3. Click **Add**

### Editing / Deleting:
- Use the **Edit** or **Delete** buttons on each testimonial card

## ⚙️ Managing Services

## ✏️ Editing Site Content

This is the most powerful section — it lets you change the **text and images** across your website without touching any code.

### How it works:
- Click **"Content"** in the sidebar
- You'll see **three tabs**: Hero Section, About, and Contact Info
- Edit any field and click **"Save Changes"**

### Hero Section (Homepage banner)

| Field | What It Controls |
|-------|-----------------|
| **tagline** | Small text above the headline (e.g., "Visual Storytelling Agency") |
| **headline** | The big bold text (e.g., "Phaedra Films") |
| **subtitle** | The descriptive text below the headline |
| **cta_text** | The text on the main button (e.g., "Book a Session") |
| **hero_image** | The main image on the homepage — click **Upload Image** to change it |

### About Section

| Field | What It Controls |
|-------|-----------------|
| **name** | Your name displayed on the About page |
| **creator_title** | The heading (e.g., "Hi, I'm Fatimah") |
| **creator_bio** | Your bio/story paragraph |
| **about_description** | Short description shown in the About section on the homepage |
| **headshot_image** | Your photo — click **Upload Image** to change it |

### Contact Info

| Field | What It Controls |
|-------|-----------------|
| **email** | Your contact email |
| **phone** | Your phone number |
| **instagram** | Your Instagram handle |
| **whatsapp** | Your WhatsApp number (used for the WhatsApp chat link) |
| **location** | Where you're based |

> **💡 Tip:** After saving changes in any section, the website updates automatically — no need to do anything else!

## 🖼️ Uploading Images

When you see an **"Upload Image"** button:
1. Click the button
2. Select an image from your device
3. Wait for it to upload (you'll see a confirmation)
4. Click **"Save Changes"** to apply

To **remove** an uploaded image, click the **X** button on the image preview, then save.

**Supported formats:** JPG, PNG, WebP  
**Recommended sizes:**
- Hero image: 1200×800px or larger (landscape)
- Headshot: 600×600px or larger (square works best)

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "I can't log in"
- Double-check your email and password
- Use "Forgot password?" to reset it
- Make sure you're going to `/admin/login` (not just `/admin`)

### "My changes aren't showing on the website"
- Make sure you clicked **"Save Changes"** after editing
- Try refreshing the public page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
- Changes appear instantly — no delay

### "Image upload isn't working"
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure the file is an image (JPG, PNG, WebP)
- Try a smaller file if the image is very large (keep under 5MB)

### "I see an error message"
- Note the exact error message
- Try refreshing the page
- If it persists, contact your developer

### "The website looks broken on mobile"
- The site is fully responsive — try clearing your browser cache
- If a specific section looks off, it may need a content adjustment (text too long, image too small, etc.)

## 🔒 Security Notes

- **Only you** have admin access — your account is protected by email + password authentication
- Your admin role is verified server-side (not stored in the browser), so it cannot be faked
- All data is stored securely in the cloud with row-level security policies
- Visitors can only **view** content and **submit** contact messages — they cannot edit or delete anything
- **Never share your admin password** with anyone you don't trust
- Change your password regularly from the Account page
